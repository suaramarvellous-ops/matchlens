Match analysis

Choose “Analyse” on a match to see a transparent estimate.
Probabilities are model estimates based on limited match data—not guarantees. Do not use them as a substitute for your own judgement.

What the model considers

Recent results

Recent goals scored and conceded when team match history is available.

Home advantage

A small home-field adjustment is applied to expected goals.

Live match state

For matches in progress, the current score and remaining time influence the estimate.

Important: no single page can reliably gather “all data online.” Injuries, lineups, tactical changes, weather and live event feeds may be missing or delayed. This starter uses the data available from the connected provider.
